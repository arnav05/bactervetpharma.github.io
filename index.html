About Us

BacterVet Pharma LLP is a forward-looking veterinary healthcare company engaged in the trading, distribution, and marketing of high-quality veterinary medicines, feed supplements, and pet healthcare products.

We are committed to strengthening animal health and productivity by delivering reliable, science-backed solutions. Through efficient supply chain management and deep market understanding, we ensure timely availability of essential products across our network.
